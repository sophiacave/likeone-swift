You already know what looks good and what looks bad. You can tell when a website feels professional versus amateur. The challenge is translating that gut feeling into words AI can act on.

Here is the design vocabulary that gets results. These are the words that turn "make it look nice" into something AI can actually build:

SPACE
White space / breathing room
"Add more white space between sections." "Give the content room to breathe." The number one difference between amateur and professional design is space.
COLOR
Color palette descriptions
"Dark mode with purple accents." "Warm, earthy tones — soft cream background, terracotta accent." "High contrast, black and white with one bold blue."
SHAPE
Corners, borders, and shapes
"Rounded corners on all cards." "Subtle borders, very light gray." "Pill-shaped buttons." These small details create the overall feeling.
TYPE
Typography and text
"Clean sans-serif font." "Large, bold headings with lighter body text." "Use a monospace font for data/numbers." Typography is 50% of good design.
Copy These

Design prompts that produce beautiful results.

Design Prompt Templates

The "Like Apple" prompt When you want clean, minimal, and premium.
PROMPT: "Style this like an Apple product page. Minimal design, lots of white space, clean sans-serif font, subtle shadows on cards, smooth hover animations. Color palette: white background, dark gray text, one accent color (blue)."
The "Dark Mode" prompt When you want moody, modern, and technical.
PROMPT: "Dark mode design. Background: very dark gray (not pure black). Text: light gray with white for headings. Accent color: electric purple. Cards with subtle borders, not shadows. Monospace font for data. Rounded corners everywhere."
The "Warm & Friendly" prompt When you want approachable and human.
PROMPT: "Warm, friendly design. Off-white background with soft shadows. Rounded corners on everything. Color palette: warm cream, soft orange accents, dark brown text. Use a rounded sans-serif font. Add gentle hover transitions. It should feel like a cozy coffee shop, not a corporate tool."
The "Reference Site" prompt When you have a specific site in mind.
PROMPT: "Style this similar to [website name]. I specifically like their: spacing, color palette, and card layout. Keep the same overall aesthetic but use [your accent color] instead of theirs." You can also screenshot a site and share the image with AI tools that support image input.
Quick Wins

5 design patterns that make anything look professional.

1. Consistent spacing

Tell AI: "Use consistent spacing throughout. Same padding in all cards, same gap between sections." Inconsistent spacing is the #1 thing that makes an app look amateur.

2. Maximum width on content

Tell AI: "Content should not stretch wider than 800px on desktop. Center it on the page." Text that stretches across the entire screen is hard to read and looks cheap.

3. Hover states on interactive elements

Tell AI: "Add hover effects to all buttons and clickable cards. Subtle color change and maybe a slight lift/shadow." This makes apps feel responsive and alive.

4. One accent color only

Tell AI: "Use one accent color for all interactive elements — buttons, links, active states. Everything else should be neutral (grays, whites, blacks)." One color = clean. Five colors = chaos.

5. Mobile-first

Tell AI: "Make sure this looks good on mobile. Stack elements vertically on small screens. Make touch targets at least 44px." More than half your users will see your app on a phone first.

The key insight: Design is not about artistic talent. It is about vocabulary. Learn 20 design words — white space, rounded corners, accent color, hover state — and you can direct AI to build beautiful interfaces. You are the art director. AI is the graphic designer.

Review

Key concepts to remember.

Design for Vibe Coders

What is white space?
Empty space between elements. It gives content room to breathe. The #1 thing that makes design look professional.
How do you tell AI about colors?
Use descriptions: "warm cream background, terracotta accent, dark brown text" or "dark mode with electric purple accents." Mood words work.
How many accent colors should you use?
One. Use it for buttons, links, and interactive elements. Keep everything else neutral (grays, whites, blacks).
What does mobile-first mean?
Design for phone screens first, then adapt for desktop. More than half your users will see your app on mobile.
What is the max width rule?
Content should not stretch wider than about 800px on desktop. Center it on the page. Text that stretches edge-to-edge is hard to read.
How can you reference a design you like?
Tell AI: "Style this similar to [website name]. I like their spacing, colors, and card layout." You can also share screenshots.
