Master an inbox is an energy vampire. step by step.

For people with limited energy, a full inbox is not just annoying — it is debilitating. Each email requires reading, processing, deciding, and responding. When you have 20 unread messages, that is 20 decision points draining your limited battery.

When you are in a depressive episode, a flare-up, or a bad pain day, emails pile up. Then the pile creates anxiety about the pile. Then the anxiety makes it even harder to deal with the pile. AI breaks this cycle.

Drafting

Email Templates for Hard Situations

Canceling an appointment You are too sick to go but feel guilty canceling.
PROMPT: "Write a brief, polite email canceling my [appointment type] on [date] with [person/office]. I need to reschedule for next week. Do not over-explain or apologize excessively. Just professional and brief." AI writes it in 10 seconds. No guilt spiral required.
Following up on something overdue You missed a deadline and need to explain without oversharing about your health.
PROMPT: "Write a professional email to [person] following up on [thing] that I was supposed to send by [date]. I was dealing with a health matter. Brief apology, attach the thing now, and move forward. Do not be overly apologetic." Professional, honest, no oversharing.
Requesting an accommodation You need a workplace or school accommodation and do not know how to ask.
PROMPT: "Write a formal email requesting a disability accommodation. I need [specific accommodation]. I have [condition — only as much as you want to share]. Address it to [person/HR]. Reference ADA/reasonable accommodation. Professional, clear, firm but polite." AI knows the right language for accommodation requests.
Responding to a confusing medical bill You got a bill that does not match what you expected.
PROMPT: "Write an email to [billing department] questioning a charge of [$amount] on [date]. I expected this to be covered by insurance. Ask them to review the claim and provide an itemized statement. Firm but professional." Clear, assertive, gets results.
Email you are dreading Any email that has been sitting in your drafts because you do not know what to say.
PROMPT: "I need to email [person] about [situation]. Here is what I want to communicate: [your key points in messy, informal language]. Write this as a professional email. [Tone: warm/formal/firm/apologetic]. Keep it under [length]." Turn your rough thoughts into a polished email.
Triage

Sort 50 emails in 5 minutes.

When your inbox is overwhelming, do not read every email. Let AI triage for you.

Inbox triage prompt
Here are my unread emails (subject lines and senders):

[paste the list]

Sort them into 3 categories:
1. URGENT — needs response today
2. THIS WEEK — important but not urgent
3. IGNORE — newsletters, promotions, FYI-only

For each URGENT email, draft a brief response I can
send immediately.
Low-energy inbox strategy

On bad days, only deal with URGENT emails. On medium days, handle URGENT and THIS WEEK. On good days, clear the backlog. AI does the sorting so you spend your energy only on what matters today.

Staying Visible

Maintain professional presence even during bad periods.

One of the cruelest aspects of episodic disabilities is that the world does not pause when you do. Clients expect responses. Employers expect participation. But you can build systems that maintain your presence even during bad periods.

Auto-responder templates

Ask AI to write professional auto-responders: "I am currently dealing with a health matter and may be slower to respond. For urgent items, please [alternative contact]. I will respond to all messages by [date]." Set it up during a good period so it is ready when you need it.

Batch processing

Instead of checking email throughout the day (energy death by a thousand cuts), batch process once or twice a day. Open inbox, paste subject lines to AI for triage, respond to urgent items, close inbox. Total time: 15 minutes instead of constant anxiety.

The key insight: Email is not just communication. For disabled people, it is often the primary interface with the systems that control your life — healthcare, insurance, employment, benefits. AI makes that interface manageable regardless of how much energy you have today.

Pre-built auto-responders, batch processing, and AI-drafted responses. Build the systems during good periods so they run during bad ones.
