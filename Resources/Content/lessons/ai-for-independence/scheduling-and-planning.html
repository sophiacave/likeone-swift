Executive function is the brain's ability to plan, organize, prioritize, start tasks, and follow through. For people with ADHD, bipolar disorder, autism, traumatic brain injury, chronic fatigue, and many other conditions, executive function is unreliable. Not because of laziness — because of neurology.

When your executive function is impaired, even simple tasks feel impossibly complex. "Schedule a dentist appointment" becomes: find the number, call (anxiety), navigate the phone tree, pick a time, remember to write it down, remember you wrote it down, actually go. Each step requires executive function you may not have.

AI becomes your external executive function. It breaks tasks into steps, reminds you what to do next, and handles the logistics you cannot face.

Task Breakdown

Turn overwhelming tasks into tiny, doable steps.

Task breakdown prompt — use for any overwhelming task
I need to [task]. Break this down into the smallest
possible steps. Each step should take less than 5
minutes and require the minimum possible energy.

Assume I have low energy and executive dysfunction.
Do not combine steps. Do not assume I will remember
the next step — list every single one.

Start with the very first physical action I need to
take (like "pick up phone" or "open browser").

Here is what that looks like for a real task:

Task Breakdown Examples

Overwhelming task: "Schedule a doctor appointment"
AI breaks it into: 1. Pick up your phone 2. Open the phone app 3. Search for [doctor office name] 4. Tap the number to call 5. When they answer, say: "I need to schedule an appointment with Dr. [name]" 6. They will ask for your name and DOB. Give it. 7. They will offer times. Pick one. 8. Write the date and time in your calendar app 9. Set a reminder for 1 day before 10. Done. Put the phone down. Each step: under 1 minute.
Overwhelming task: "File for disability benefits"
AI breaks it into: 1. Open browser 2. Go to ssa.gov 3. Click "Apply for Disability" 4. Create an account (email + password) 5. Stop here if tired. Save login info. Day 2: 6. Log back in 7. Start application — personal info section 8. Use your personal info template (from Lesson 3) 9. Stop after personal info if tired. (Continues in manageable chunks across multiple days)
Overwhelming task: "Clean the kitchen"
AI breaks it into: 1. Stand in the kitchen doorway 2. Pick up any trash you can see. Put it in the trash can. 3. Sit down if you need to. 4. Put any dishes in the sink 5. Stop if tired. This counts as progress. 6. If continuing: fill the sink with soapy water 7. Wash 5 dishes. Just 5. 8. Wipe one counter. 9. Done for now. Each step is completable. Stopping is always allowed.
Routines

Build routines that flex with your energy.

Rigid routines fail for people with variable energy. You need routines that adapt to how you feel today — not how you felt yesterday or how you hope to feel tomorrow.

Energy-aware routine prompt
Create a daily routine with 3 versions:

LOW ENERGY DAY: Only absolute essentials. Meds,
water, one meal, one basic hygiene task. Nothing
else required. Everything else is bonus.

MEDIUM ENERGY DAY: Essentials plus 2-3 productive
tasks. Email triage, one appointment, light chores.
Include rest breaks between tasks.

HIGH ENERGY DAY: Full productive day. All tasks from
medium plus bigger projects, errands, social
interaction. But still include rest periods.

Each version should feel achievable, not aspirational.
On a low day, completing the low list IS a full day.
Appointments

Never miss an appointment again.

AI appointment script

Dreading a phone call? Ask AI to write you a script: "Write a phone script for calling [office] to [schedule/cancel/reschedule] an appointment. Include what to say when they answer, what information they will probably ask for, and how to handle being put on hold." Read the script. Make the call. Cross it off.

Online booking first

Before calling, ask AI: "Does [doctor/office name] have online appointment booking?" Many offices now let you book online, skipping the phone call entirely. AI can help you find the booking link.

Reminder system

Ask AI to create a reminder schedule: "Set up reminders for my appointment on [date]: 1 week before, 1 day before, and 2 hours before. Include the address and what I need to bring." Put these in your calendar app.

Scheduling and Planning

What is executive function?

How does AI serve as external executive function?

What is an energy-aware routine?
A routine with three versions: low energy (essentials only), medium energy (essentials + 2-3 tasks), high energy (full productive day). Each version feels achievable.
How should you handle phone call dread?

What makes a good task breakdown?
Smallest possible steps. Under 5 minutes each. Start with the first physical action. List every step. Stopping at any point is always allowed.
