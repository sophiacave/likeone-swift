The Routine

Your morning AI routine (5 minutes).

Review inbox Plan day Draft emails Organize tasks

The people who get the most value from Claude aren't the ones who use it for one big task. They're the ones who use it for 5 small tasks every single day. Here's the morning routine that makes it habit.

The 4-Step Morning Routine

Step 1: Inbox Triage (90 seconds) Open Claude. Paste your top 3 emails. Ask: Which need a reply today, and draft the most urgent one.
Why this works: You walk into your day with the hardest email handled. The psychological weight of a full inbox drops immediately. Most people save 15 minutes on this step alone.
Step 2: Daily Plan (60 seconds) Paste your calendar and to-do list. Ask: What should I prioritize today? Where are the gaps for deep work?
Why this works: Claude sees your day holistically. It spots conflicts, suggests reordering, and protects your focus time. Better than any to-do app.
Step 3: Meeting Prep (90 seconds) For your first meeting: paste the agenda and attendees. Ask: What should I prepare? What questions might come up?
Why this works: 90 seconds of prep with Claude makes you the most prepared person in every room. The difference between leading a meeting and surviving one.
Step 4: One Creative Task (60 seconds) Pick one thing you have been putting off. Give Claude the context. Let it generate a starting point.
Why this works: Procrastination usually comes from blank-page paralysis. Claude gives you something to react to — and reacting is 10x easier than creating from scratch.

Customize It

Make the routine yours — real customization examples.

The morning routine above is a starting template. Here is how different professionals customize it for their specific needs.

Customization by Role

Sales Manager Your inbox is full of prospect replies, your calendar is back-to-back calls, and you need pipeline updates.
CUSTOMIZATION: Step 1 (Inbox): Focus on hot leads only. Ask Claude to rank by deal size and urgency. Step 2 (Daily Plan): Have Claude identify which calls need prep and which are routine check-ins. Step 3 (Meeting Prep): Paste the prospect CRM notes. Ask for objection handling and talking points. Step 4 (Creative): Draft a follow-up sequence for the deal that stalled last week.
Executive Assistant You manage someone else calendar, handle their inbox overflow, and coordinate across teams.
CUSTOMIZATION: Step 1 (Inbox): Triage your boss inbox. Flag urgent, draft replies for routine ones. Step 2 (Daily Plan): Map your boss day. Identify conflicts, prep materials for each meeting. Step 3 (Meeting Prep): Create briefing docs for your boss — attendees, context, goals. Step 4 (Creative): Draft the weekly team update email your boss never has time to write.
Freelancer / Solopreneur You wear every hat. Marketing, client work, invoicing, and admin all compete for attention.
CUSTOMIZATION: Step 1 (Inbox): Separate client emails from admin. Draft client replies first. Step 2 (Daily Plan): Block revenue-generating work in the morning. Push admin to afternoon. Step 3 (Meeting Prep): For client calls, paste project notes and ask for status summary + talking points. Step 4 (Creative): Draft this week social media post or blog outline.
Teacher / Professor Your mornings involve lesson planning, student emails, and grading deadlines.
CUSTOMIZATION: Step 1 (Inbox): Focus on student questions and parent emails. Draft empathetic, clear replies. Step 2 (Daily Plan): Map class schedule + grading deadlines. Identify prep gaps. Step 3 (Meeting Prep): For parent conferences, paste student data and ask for talking points. Step 4 (Creative): Generate a discussion question or warm-up activity for today class.
Setup Guide

Build your assistant in 15 minutes — step by step.

Follow these steps in order. By the end, you will have a fully configured personal AI assistant ready to save you hours every week.

1
Set your custom instructions (3 minutes)
Go to Claude settings. Paste the template from Lesson 7 (or grab one from the flashcards above). Fill in the brackets with your actual role, company, tools, and preferences. Save.
2
Create your first Project (3 minutes)
Pick your most important recurring task — client management, weekly reports, or a specific project. Create a Claude Project, add relevant documents and context. Now every conversation in that project is pre-loaded.
3
Save your prompt library (4 minutes)
Copy the 8 prompts from the library section below. Save them in a note app, bookmark, or document you can access quickly. Customize the brackets for your job.
4
Do the morning routine once right now (5 minutes)
Open Claude. Run all four steps with real content from your day. Copy the inbox triage prompt, paste your actual emails, and see the result. This is how you build the habit — by doing it, not just reading about it.

Important: Do not skip step 4. Reading about the routine is not the same as doing it. The people who get the most value from this course are the ones who actually open Claude and paste their first prompt today — not "someday."

Templates

Ready-to-paste templates for common situations.

Beyond the morning routine, here are templates for situations that come up throughout your workweek. Copy these into your prompt library.

Template — End of Week Review (Friday, 3 minutes)
Here is what I accomplished this week:
[list your wins, tasks completed, progress made]

And here is what I did not finish:
[list incomplete tasks and why]

Write a brief self-review: what went well, what
I should do differently next week, and my top 3
priorities for Monday morning.
Template — Difficult Conversation Prep
I need to have a difficult conversation with
[person and relationship].

The situation: [what happened]
My goal: [what I want to achieve]
My concern: [what I am worried about]

Give me: opening line, 3 key points to make,
potential objections and how to handle them,
and a strong closing statement. Keep it
respectful and solution-oriented.
Template — Learn Something New (anytime)
I need to understand [topic] for [reason].

My current knowledge level: [beginner/some
familiarity/intermediate]

Explain it in 3 levels:
1. The 30-second version (ELI5)
2. The 3-minute version (with key terms)
3. The deep dive (with examples and nuance)

Use everyday analogies. No jargon unless you
define it first.
