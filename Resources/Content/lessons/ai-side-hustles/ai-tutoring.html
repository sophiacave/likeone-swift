Here's a wild stat: 77% of companies say AI is a priority, but only 15% have actually implemented it in their workflows. That gap between intent and action is worth billions. And you don't need a PhD to fill it. You just need to be further along the AI curve than the person paying you.

Think about it. A real estate agent who spends 3 hours writing listing descriptions could do it in 15 minutes with AI. A marketing manager who takes a week to create a content calendar could do it in an afternoon. A lawyer who manually reviews 200-page contracts could flag key clauses in minutes. Each of these professionals will pay $200-$500/hour for someone to show them how.

You don't need to know everything about AI. You need to know more than your client, understand their specific workflow, and translate AI capabilities into their language. That's the whole business.

Hot Industries

These five industries will pay you the most to teach them AI.

1. Real Estate ($150-$300/session): Listing descriptions, market analysis, lead follow-up emails, social content. Agents make $100K+ and gladly invest $500/month in training that saves them 10+ hours/week.

2. Legal ($200-$500/session): Contract review, legal research summaries, client communication drafts, case brief analysis. Lawyers bill $300-$700/hour, so paying you $500 for an hour that saves them 5 hours is a 10x return.

3. Marketing & Agencies ($150-$400/session): Campaign ideation, copy generation, A/B test variants, analytics interpretation, social media management. Agencies with 5-20 employees see the biggest ROI from team-wide AI training.

4. Healthcare Administration ($150-$300/session): Not clinical AI — administrative AI. Patient communication, scheduling optimization, insurance pre-authorization letters, medical record summaries. Massive demand, very few trainers.

5. Small Business Owners ($100-$250/session): Email marketing, bookkeeping prompts, customer service automation, inventory descriptions, social media content. They're generalists who need AI applied across their entire business.

Packaging Your Service

Don't sell hours. Sell transformations.

Nobody wants to buy "AI tutoring." They want to buy "Save 10 hours a week" or "Write listing descriptions in 5 minutes instead of 45." Your packaging determines your price ceiling.

Three service tiers that work:
  • Quick Start ($150-$300): A single 90-minute session. Audit their workflow, identify the top 3 AI opportunities, set up tools, and create custom prompts they can use immediately.
  • Deep Dive ($500-$1,500): A 4-week program. Weekly 60-minute sessions, custom prompt library, workflow documentation, and async support via Slack or Voxer. This is your bread and butter.
  • Team Training ($2,000-$5,000): Half-day or full-day workshop for teams of 5-20. Hands-on exercises, custom playbook for their industry, follow-up resources. One day of work, premium price.

Pro tip: Always lead with a discovery call (15-20 minutes, free). Ask about their daily tasks, biggest time sinks, and current tool stack. This lets you customize your pitch and demonstrate expertise before they ever pay a dollar.

Delivery Framework

The AAAA Method: Audit, Automate, Accelerate, Anchor.

Use this four-step framework for every client engagement:

Audit: Map their current workflow. Where do they spend the most time? What's repetitive? What requires creativity vs. execution? Screen-share and watch them work for 15 minutes. You'll spot AI opportunities they can't see.

Automate: Set up the tools. Create accounts, install extensions, configure API keys if needed. Build their first 5-10 custom prompts live, explaining the logic. They need to see the magic happen in real-time.

Accelerate: Have them do it themselves while you watch. Correct their prompt engineering in real-time. This is where the real learning happens — they move from observer to practitioner in 20 minutes.

Anchor: Document everything. Give them a custom playbook (Google Doc or Notion) with their prompts, workflows, and cheat sheets. This becomes their reference and your proof of value. Clients who have a playbook retain 80% of what you taught. Clients who don't retain 20%.

Scaling With Groups

One-to-many is where the real money lives.

1:1 tutoring caps your income at the number of hours you can work. Group workshops break that ceiling. A workshop for 15 people at $200/person = $3,000 for a half-day of work. That's $3,000 for the same effort as a $300 individual session.

Where to find groups: local chambers of commerce, industry associations, coworking spaces, LinkedIn events, and corporate HR departments. Cold email template: "I help [industry] professionals save 10+ hours/week with AI tools. I offer team workshops — would this be valuable for your members?"

Record your workshops (with permission) and repurpose them into online courses, YouTube content, and social media clips. One live workshop generates 20+ pieces of content that drive future sales.
