CRISIS RESOURCES — SAVE THESE:

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988 (24/7, free, confidential). Supports anyone in suicidal crisis, emotional distress, or substance use crisis. Available in English and Spanish. For Deaf/Hard of Hearing: use your preferred relay service or dial 711 then 988.

Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741 (24/7, free, confidential). Connects you with a trained crisis counselor via text. For anyone in any type of crisis.

Trevor Project (LGBTQ+ youth): Call 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678 (24/7).

Trans Lifeline: Call 877-565-8860 (staffed by trans people, for trans people).

Veterans Crisis Line: Call 988, then press 1. Or text 838255.

AI Cannot Handle This

The line between AI help and human help.

AI is NOT equipped to handle mental health crises. This is not a limitation we're working on — it's a fundamental boundary. Here's why:

  • AI cannot assess risk. It cannot tell if you're in immediate danger. A trained crisis counselor can.
  • AI cannot call for help. If you need emergency services, AI cannot dispatch them. A hotline can.
  • AI cannot provide the human connection that saves lives. In a crisis, knowing a real person cares matters. AI's empathy is simulated.
  • AI can say the wrong thing. AI responses are generated by patterns, not clinical training. In a crisis, the wrong response can be dangerous.
  • AI conversations may not be private. Your crisis disclosure might be stored and reviewed. Crisis hotlines are legally confidential.
The hard rule: If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, thinking about suicide or self-harm, or experiencing a psychiatric emergency — close the AI, and call 988 or 911. This is not negotiable.
Recognizing a Crisis

Signs it's time to reach for human help.

Not every hard day is a crisis. But some are. Here's how to know the difference:

In yourself:

  • Thoughts of suicide or self-harm, even if they feel "passive" or "not serious"
  • Feeling like others would be better off without you
  • Giving away possessions or saying goodbye to people
  • Sudden calm after a period of intense depression (this can signal a plan has been made)
  • Feeling completely hopeless with no ability to see a future
  • Hearing or seeing things that aren't there
  • Unable to care for yourself — not eating, not sleeping for days, not functioning

In someone you care about:

  • Talking about wanting to die or being a burden
  • Withdrawing from everyone suddenly
  • Dramatic mood changes — especially sudden calmness after depression
  • Increased substance use
  • Researching methods or accessing means

If any of these apply: call 988, text HOME to 741741, or go to an emergency room. You are not overreacting.

Finding Professional Help

Beyond crisis — getting ongoing support.

Crisis lines are for emergencies. But you also deserve ongoing support. AI can actually help you find it:

Ask AI to help you search: "Help me find a therapist in [your city] who specializes in [your concern] and accepts [your insurance]." AI can help you navigate the confusing landscape of finding a provider.

Sliding scale and free options: Ask AI about community mental health centers, university training clinics (lower cost, supervised students), Open Path Collective (sessions for $30-$80), and SAMHSA's treatment locator.

Overcoming barriers: Tell AI what's stopping you — cost, stigma, time, not knowing where to start — and ask for specific solutions. Sometimes the barrier is just the phone call. AI can even help you script what to say when you call a therapist's office for the first time.

You deserve help. Seeking professional support is not weakness. It's the same as seeing a doctor for a broken bone. Your brain is part of your body, and it deserves care too.
